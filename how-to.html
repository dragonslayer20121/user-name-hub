How to use
the hub

Welcome to your portal. Here's how to explore the themes, open the anime character screen, visit profiles, and play Shadow Hunt.

01 // THEMES

Switch your anime world

Use the theme dropdown in the top navigation to switch between Shadow Monarch, Naruto, Black Clover, and Bleach. The colours and effects adapt to your choice.

02 // CHARACTER PORTAL

Click the logo

Click the DRAGONSLAYER // HUB logo to open the full-screen character portal for your selected theme. Use the close button or press Escape to return.

03 // SOUND & EFFECTS

Customize the atmosphere

Use MUSIC to toggle the ambient soundtrack, SFX to enable or disable interface sounds, and SHADOWS to turn the ember particles on or off. Music may need a click to start because of browser audio rules.

04 // PROFILES

Explore your links

Choose EXPLORE PROFILES to jump to your profile cards. Use Visit portal to open a linked profile, or Copy username to copy its displayed username. Some profiles may not have a link configured yet.

05 // SHADOW HUNT

Play the mini-game

Enter a name, start the round, then click the glowing shadow orb as quickly as you can. You have 30 seconds. Your score is saved to this browser's local leaderboard.

06 // SECRET AWAKENING

Find the hidden effect

On the main hub, click the large symbol above the title to trigger a short theme-specific transformation effect. You can also focus it and press Enter or Space.

Quick tip: If the latest update doesn't appear, refresh the hub with Ctrl + F5. The Shadow Hunt leaderboard is currently local to each browser, so it isn't a shared global leaderboard.
